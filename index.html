Navigation Controls

  • Pan: Click and drag, swipe your touchpad, or push your mouse to the left/right screen edges. Keyboard arrows also work.
  • Zoom: Use your mouse scroll wheel or two-finger touchpad swipe.
    Read: Click any English translation to open its historical context.
  • Pan: 1-finger swipe to pan horizontally. 2-finger swipe to pan freely.
    Zoom: 2-finger pinch.
  • Read: Tap on any English translation to open its historical context.
  • Zoom in closely on a specific scene to automatically load ultra-high resolution textures.
